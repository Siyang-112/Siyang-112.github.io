Voice Personal Assistant — VUI & Interaction Design

Role:HMI Designer
Company:CapsuleTech
Client:The Durant Guild / GM Group
Duration:2022 — 2023

Localized and redesigned the Voice Personal Assistant (VPA) for the Chinese market, optimizing the voice corpus, visual feedback, and dynamic effects to match local user habits and expectations for automotive voice interaction.

Challenge

The original Google Assistant-based VPA didn't resonate with Chinese consumers — different speech patterns, expectations for proactive assistance, and visual feedback preferences. The challenge was to create a localized voice experience that feels native while maintaining technical compatibility.

Approach

  • Researched Chinese consumer voice interaction habits and expectations
  • Optimized voice corpus to replace Google Assistant patterns with local idioms
  • Designed new voice avatar images and dynamic visual effects
  • Validated through market research and user testing
  • Created interactive specifications for developer handoff

Key Deliverables

  • Localized voice corpus
  • VUI flow diagrams
  • Voice avatar visual design
  • Dynamic effects animation specs
  • Interactive specifications
  • User testing validation
