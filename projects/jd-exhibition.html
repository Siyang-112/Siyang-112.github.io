JD West & South HQ Exhibition Hall

Role:Project Manager
Company:Seekers
Client:JD.com
Duration:2021.06 — 2021.09

Managed the complete design and construction of the exhibition hall at JD's West and South Headquarters — from concept through physical build, coordinating designers, construction teams, and stakeholders to deliver on time.

Challenge

A physical exhibition space project with tight deadlines, requiring seamless coordination between creative design vision and practical construction constraints. Multiple stakeholder groups with different priorities needed alignment throughout the process.

Approach

  • Negotiation stage: aligned interests across all parties
  • Design stage: translated professional design knowledge into actionable plans
  • Production stage: stayed on-site for 2 weeks to oversee construction
  • Controlled costs and maintained quality standards throughout
