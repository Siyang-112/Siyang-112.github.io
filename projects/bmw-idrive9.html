BMW iDrive 9 HMI Demo

Role:HMI Designer & Project Manager
Company:CapsuleTech
Client:BMW Group
Duration:2022 — 2023

Created interactive demos of the BMW iDrive 9 HMI system using Protopie, covering PHUD, Launcher, multimedia, and vehicle settings modules. Successfully demonstrated at the BMW Developer Conference in Munich.

Challenge

BMW needed high-fidelity interactive demonstrations of the next-generation iDrive 9 system for their Developer Conference. The demos had to showcase multi-screen interaction, module linkage, and the full interaction depth of a production system — all built in Protopie with pixel-perfect accuracy.

Approach

  • Built interactive prototypes in Protopie for PHUD, Launcher, multimedia, and settings
  • Achieved multi-screen and multi-module linkage demonstration
  • Coordinated team of 10 people, communicating with BMW clients in English
  • Delivered on schedule for the Munich Developer Conference
